## What the numbers say

1. **The fusion costs here.** Kept less frozen is -0.0608 (word by the rules: "costs"); in all 2000 resamplings the kept system is
   below the frozen recipe (interval -0.0832 to -0.0413). On the development set the same fusion read +0.0027 ("no clear difference").
2. **The second view is the cause.** Alone it scores 0.4474 with a target line of 0.3489, under chance: on the target domain of
   these new machine types it ranks normal clips as more anomalous than the anomalies (BandSealer 0.2408, CoffeeGrinder 0.2476,
   ToyRCCar 0.2570, HomeCamera 0.2960). The fusion carries that into the kept system (target line 0.4375 against the frozen recipe's 0.5541).
3. **The frozen recipe alone holds up.** 0.5922 would stand at place 7; it is at or above 56.51 in 1960 of 2000 resamplings and at
   or above 57.23 in 1880.
4. **Attribute classes help the frozen recipe on the evaluation set, and the development set said the opposite.** Evaluation:
   0.6228 against 0.5922 (above in 1997 of 2000; interval of the difference +0.0108 to +0.0513). Development: 0.5528 against 0.5685.
   The 0.6228 is above the first team's 61.63 %, and stays above it in 1317 of 2000 resamplings, but nothing in the rules or on the
   development set selected this system. It cannot be reported as the result.
5. **The development set did not predict the evaluation set**, in either direction: it rated the fusion neutral (it costs 6 points)
   and attribute classes for the frozen recipe as worse (they are 3 points better). This is the first-shot setting doing what it is
   built to do: the evaluation machine types are all new.

## Where my expectations were wrong

- I expected the result near the baselines, about 56 %, and said I had no confidence. It is 53.14 %, three points lower, and below
  the baselines in 1994 of 2000 resamplings.
- On DCASE 2022 the fusion helped (+0.0177). I carried it into 2025 as part of the kept system. Here it is the reason for the low score.

## Limits

- Not an entry; one evaluation; the labels were public while the system was built, though the rules and the choice were fixed first.
- Gate G-DEV was changed twice after stops, the second time with the development numbers known. The change let the run continue; it
  did not change any score or the choice.
- Clips shorter than 10 s were repeated to fill 10 s (AutoTrash, CoffeeGrinder, HomeCamera, ToyRCCar: 1200 clips each); BandSealer
  is 24-bit and was stored as 16-bit. The challenge teams were free to treat these differently.
- 100 anomalies and 50 + 50 normal clips per type: the intervals above are wide (kept: sd 0.0125), and places move with them
  (the kept system's place ranges mostly between 25 and 30).
- The hidden attributes of the evaluator repository were saved with the ground truth but not used.
