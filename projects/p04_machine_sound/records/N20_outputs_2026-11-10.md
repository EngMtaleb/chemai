## Looked after (seen after the results; not decisions)
- **N2N-MWF is strong on confirmation types but failed the development bar.** Confirmation 0.9089 against 0.8466.
  - With BEATs LDN1, valves rise to 0.9264 / 0.9308 and slide rails to 0.9929 / 0.9054.
  - It is a candidate only under a new rule written before a new run, never by re-reading these numbers.
- **LDN1 against B1, over the 32 cells (2 encoders × 4 types × 4 inputs).** This is against the expectation written before running (that LDN1 would help the B splits most).
  - Split A: mean gain +0.028 (23 of 32 up). Example: BEATs fan CH0 A 0.7255 → 0.8078.
  - B+10: mean gain +0.019 (22 of 32 up). But single-microphone slide rails B+10 fell 0.7822 → 0.6331 (BEATs).
  - Split B (no clips of the machine itself): mean change −0.082 (only 4 of 32 up). 16 of 32 LDN1 B averages are below 0.5, against 8 of 32 for B1.
  - So LDN1 needs some normal clips of the machine itself in the reference. DCASE 2020 provides them (each test machine has its own normal training clips), so it suits the single-microphone benchmark.
- **SEG scoring did not help valves.** BEATs valve N2N-CH0 A: SEG-max 0.8227 against B1 0.8916. This is against the expectation.
- **Remaining gaps against the program's targets:**
  - Valves on one microphone (0.876 against 0.979).
  - Pumps on one microphone (0.856 against 0.898).
  - New-machine fans (MWF B+10 0.883 against the 6 dB target 0.9539).
