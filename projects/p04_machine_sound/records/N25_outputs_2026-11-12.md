## Looked after (no decision rests on this)
- **Why LDN1 fails on the view: one loose reference clip on slide rail id_02.**

  - From the saved scores: 86 of the machine's 267 anomalies get an LDN1 score below the median normal clip (1.07). By B1 none does.
  - LDN1 divides a clip's distance to a reference by that reference's distance to its own nearest reference. When one reference wins both minima, B1 / LDN1 is that second distance. Its median is 0.0033 for this machine's normal test clips.
  - 89 of the anomalies have one and the same ratio, 0.1217 (equal to nine decimals). So one reference wins for all 89, and its own nearest reference is 0.1217 away, over 30 times the usual. Two references that are each other's nearest neighbour would give the same picture; the scores cannot tell these apart.
  - All 86 anomalies below the normal median are among these 89 (their LDN1 scores: 0.38 to 1.42, median 0.52). The other 178 anomalies all score above the normal median; the lowest is at 1.17.
