## Caveats
- **A third reading of the same 12 cells.** The weak cells were named with the first two in view, so reading 3 is "looked after".
- **Published MIMII numbers use another split.** The comparison is indicative, as in notebooks 23 and 24.
- **The fused system was selected on DCASE 2020's development set,** which holds the same recordings as MIMII's −6 dB level. The keep rule here is a confirmation in another split. The 6 and 0 dB levels are where the system had not been seen.
- **The view sees all four machines of a type; a fold's frozen models saw two.** Both are legitimate in Split A. It is not the like-for-like test of one recipe that readings 1 and 2 were.
- **The frozen system's spread is measured on clips its models trained on,** by the left-out score. The spreads carried from −6 dB give the same picture.
- **The fusion is not better everywhere.** Pump AUC at 6 dB and pump pAUC at every level are lower, all through pump id_02.
- **ZB and the carried spreads were declared as reported only.** Their numbers on these test clips are now known, so neither can be adopted from this run.
- **Each training ran once.** The seed table is the only measure of run-to-run variation.
- **The view serves known machines only.** Nothing changes on the program's B and B+10 splits, on ARR or on MIC+.
