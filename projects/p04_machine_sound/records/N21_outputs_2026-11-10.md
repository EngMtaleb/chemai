## Amendment 1's diagnostic: the cause was not clipping
- **What was measured.** For every processed stream, the notebook recorded the share of clips whose signal reaches |x| ≥ 1.
  - It is 0 for all 24 streams: MWF at 6 and −6 dB, both N2N-CH0 folds, and the XC clips through both denoisers, for all four types.
  - The largest peak is 0.703 (valve XC clips through denoiser fold 2). For fans, the MWF stream peaks at 0.241 and N2N-CH0 fold 1 at 0.043.
- **What this means.** By the amendment's own rule, the first attempt's failure had another cause.
- **The likely cause (looked after; not tested).**
  - Between the two attempts, the fan streams were computed by the same code. Only two things changed: the storage (int16 → float16) and a fresh store folder.
  - With no clipping, the remaining difference is 16-bit rounding (step 1/32768). It adds a white floor about 101 dB below full scale.
  - The floor can dominate mel bands where a quiet processed signal has less energy than that. Microphone 0 is exact, because it comes from 16-bit files.
- **Consequence for notebooks 19 and 20 (looked after).**
  - Notebook 19's N2N (MVDR base) and notebook 20's N2N-MWF read their array input from 16-bit streams. So they carry this rounding, not clipping.
  - Notebook 20's D2 compared N2N-MWF (16-bit input) with MWF embedded from float signals, so that comparison was not like for like.
  - N2N-MWF failed D2 on development (0.9166 against 0.9173) while gaining on confirmation (0.9089 against 0.8466).
  - Whether float input changes D2 is open. Repeating D2 on float streams is the clean check.
  - D1 (N2N-CH0, whose input is microphone 0) and D3 (the scorer) are not affected.

## Caveats
- **Seeds.**
  - Each decision used one seed. MIC's seed range on development (0.0112) is larger than the 0.01 bar.
  - So on MIC, EF's pass (+0.0110) and EP's miss (+0.0087) are within seed noise.
  - ARR's PD gain (+0.0156 / +0.0626) is far above ARR's seed range (0.0024 / 0.0031).
- **Many comparisons:** 21 ingredient decisions and 3 FF decisions.
- **DCASE 2020 compliance of MIC:**
  - The final MIC system uses BEATs and ECHO, two models outside DCASE 2020's allowed list. T3 (notebook 23) will say so.
  - MIC's Split-A training clips may include DCASE 2020 development test clips. Notebook 23 retrains on DCASE's own lists.
- **PD in practice:**
  - PD needs a pseudo-clean recording of every training segment. In MIMII, this is the 6 dB twin of the same segment.
  - In a plant, it would need a second, quieter recording of the same moment, for example a microphone close to the machine.
- **MIC+:** fine-tuning on the denoised single microphone did not beat frozen BEATs on development, with any ingredient.
