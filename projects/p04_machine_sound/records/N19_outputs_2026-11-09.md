## Looked after (seen after the results; not decisions)
- **N2N against MWF.** N2N was essentially tied with MWF on development (0.9154 against 0.9174) and clearly better on confirmation (0.9098 against 0.8466).
  - Valves A: 0.9053 against 0.7909.
  - Slide rails A: 0.9988 against 0.9027 (BEATs).
  - This motivates a pre-registered test of N2N on top of MWF in the next stage.
- **The comparison with the published numbers is not like for like.** The published methods use one microphone.
  - On one microphone our frozen pipeline (CH0) is below them for every type: pump 0.8234, fan 0.7256, valve 0.6897, slider 0.8160.
  - Notebook 16's fine-tuned CH0 is above for pumps (0.9553) and below for fans (0.8173).
  - Our lead on pumps and fans comes from the array.
- **DS lowers the broadband twin-meter SNR** below CH0 on pumps, valves and slide rails, yet raises AUC. Its gains must sit in bands that carry little power.
