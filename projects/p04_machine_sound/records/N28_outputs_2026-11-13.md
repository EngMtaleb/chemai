## What limits the reading
- **This is not an entry in the challenge.** The table is a yardstick. The encoders (BEATs, ECHO) were pretrained on large general audio sets and were published after the 2022 challenge; I have not checked what each team was allowed to use or used.
- **One evaluation set, scored once.** The resampling above covers the draw of test clips, not the draw of training seeds or of machines.
- **The system uses no domain label and no attribute,** and sets no decision threshold. The challenge's F1 columns are not measured here.
- **pAUC is the weakest line** (0.6199): at low false-alarm rates the system is far from its AUC.
- The comparison is with each team's best system on the official score, taken from the results page to two decimals.
