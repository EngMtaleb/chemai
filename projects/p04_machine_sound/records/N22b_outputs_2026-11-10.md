## Caveats
- **MIC+'s change trades valves for slide rails** (per machine, above). It passed the rule (confirmation within −0.01 on average), but the average hides it.
- **MIC's fusion uses twice as many models** (two encoders × three seeds). Part of the gain may come from the larger ensemble rather than the second encoder. That was not tested (looked after).
- **BEATs iter3's provenance:** a third party's copy, unverifiable against the authors' file (above).
- **Many comparisons:** notebook 22's ten decisions and these seven. Two passed here, N0 by +0.0051 over its bar and MIC's N2 by +0.0085 over its bar.
- **DCASE 2020 compliance of MIC:** BEATs iter3 joins BEATs and ECHO outside DCASE 2020's list of allowed models. T3 (notebook 23) is a post-challenge comparison and will say so.
