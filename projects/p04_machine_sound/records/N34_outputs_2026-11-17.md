  comes from aiming.
- It does not make a simple family as good as Family 5: the best simple family on eight microphones (F2, 0.8209) equals the
  pre-trained model alone on one microphone (0.8234). The fine-tuned expert on one microphone: pump 0.9485 (notebook 23).
- In the quiet factory (6 dB) the array changes little, and for F2 and F3 the aimed signal is slightly below one microphone
  (0.9504 against 0.9664; 0.9248 against 0.9524).
- Family 4: no effect that can be told from its own variation.
