## Caveats
- **This is a post-challenge comparison.** BEATs, BEATs iter3 and ECHO are outside DCASE 2020's list of allowed models, and the five teams built their systems in 2020. Beside a 2024 system built on BEATs (AnoPatch) the kept system is ahead on ToyCar and slightly behind on ToyConveyor.
- **The five teams are the program's selection** (official ranks 1, 3, 5, 7 and 12), not the whole results table of 117 systems. The rank among six is not an official rank.
- **The six-type summary mixes two kinds of evidence.** The two toy types were scored once here. The four MIMII types come from notebook 25, a second scoring of their evaluation set.
- **ToyConveyor's word rests on a small margin:** 0.89 points above the third of the five teams, with the resampling shares above.
- **13 classes.** Both recipes were trained on the two toy types alone. The view's paper trains the six types together.
- **ToyCar's last second is not read.** Every system read the first 10 s, as the view's reference code does.
- **Each training ran once.** GPU training is not bit-reproducible.
- **Three sets of scores were written on the evaluation set.** Only the kept system's is the result. The two parts answer question 2 and cannot replace it.
