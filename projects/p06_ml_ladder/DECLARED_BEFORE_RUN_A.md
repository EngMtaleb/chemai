# Declared before the run — Part A: will a train reach its review level within 28 days?

Written on 2026-10-27, after the development work in `stepA1_ro_dev.py` and **before any test plant
was generated or any model was scored on the real deposit**. Committed before `stepA2_ro_test.py`
runs; the script refuses to run otherwise and records the commit. Nothing above the closing line is
edited after the run.

## The question and the methods to beat

Project 3 asked each week, for each RO train: will its 7-day normalised pressure drop reach the
review level (1.15 or 1.20) within the next 28 days? On the real deposit, tested once, its forecast
caught 0.21 of the crossings at precision 0.72, and a simple margin rule (the 7-day median within
0.03 of the level) caught 0.43 at 0.93. This part asks whether machine learning does better, with
the same information: the cycle's observed DP_norm up to the decision day.

## Data and split

| Role | Data | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Development | the 30 simulated plants Project 3 developed on (seed 1001), normalised as the deposit is (vendor exponent 1.5) | 10,192 decisions, 627 cycles, 27 % positive |
| **Final exam, scored once** | **60 new simulated plants, seed 6006, generated by the test script after this commit** | — |
| Real deposit, exploratory | the 213 decisions of Project 3's external test (11 cycles, 61 crossings) | its answers were seen in 2026 |

Unit and label as in Project 3: weekly decisions from day 14 of each cycle, both levels; decisions
already above the level and decisions where the frozen forecast reports "insufficient data" are
left out. Simulation answers with the counterfactual path; the real deposit with the measured 7-day
median, and a cleaning that hid the answer drops the row. All tuning is grouped by plant.

## The ladder (frozen in `results/stepA1_ro_dev.json`)

Features: 20 plain statistics of the observed series (`PLAIN` in `chemai/features/ro_decisions.py`)
and 12 physics-informed ones (`PHYSICS`: the gap to the level over the series' noise, and what the
straight-line and gap-law forecasts of Project 3 say).

| Rung | Models | Development average precision (5-fold, grouped by plant) |
|---|---|---|
| Machine learning (plain features) | logistic regression, k-nearest neighbours, RBF SVM (Nystroem), random forest, extra trees, XGBoost, LightGBM — each tuned on a small grid | 0.585–0.627; best: **random forest 0.627** |
| Ensemble | mean probability of the three best (random forest, LightGBM, XGBoost) | 0.632 |
| Physics-informed machine learning | the same seven on plain + physics features | 0.570–0.630; best: **XGBoost 0.630** |
| Physics-informed ensemble | XGBoost, LightGBM, random forest | 0.632 |
| Deep learning | MLP on the plain features; 1-D CNN on the 42-day daily sequence + 6 scalars | MLP **0.609**, CNN 0.595 |
| Physics-informed deep learning | **GapLawNet**: the CNN predicts only the fouling rate in the gap law's straight coordinate z = DP^(−1/2.2), as a log-normal, plus an upset probability; the law turns it into P(cross within 28 days) | 0.577 |

On development the margin rule scores recall 0.37 at precision 0.561, the forecast 0.30 at 0.445.

**Threshold.** Each model's threshold is fixed here: the one at which its development
(out-of-fold) precision equals the margin rule's development precision, 0.561. At that threshold the
best development models reach recall 0.61–0.65. Thresholds are in the JSON and are not changed.

**Training for the test.** Every model is refitted on all development rows with its frozen settings
(the SVM on a 4,000-row subsample, as in tuning); each network trains once, with early stopping on a
fifth of the development plants.

## What is reported, and the pass mark

On the final exam, for every model (all seven of each ML rung, both ensembles, three networks) and
for the two methods to beat:

- recall, precision and F1 at the frozen threshold; average precision;
- recall minus the margin rule's recall, with a 95 % interval from resampling whole plants.

**A rung beats the margin rule** if its frozen pick (random forest; the ensemble; XGBoost; its
ensemble; the MLP; GapLawNet) has a recall above the rule's with the 95 % interval above zero, at a
precision no more than 0.05 below the rule's. Otherwise the result is reported as a tie or a loss.

## The real deposit — exploratory, no pass mark

The same frozen models and thresholds, on the 213 real decisions, against the forecast (0.21 / 0.72)
and the margin rule (0.43 / 0.93); 95 % intervals from resampling whole cycles. The simulator is
known to be noisier, with cycles 2.8 times longer, than this plant (Project 3, step 3). Also run,
also exploratory: the frozen pick of each ML rung re-tuned and trained on 30 plants of the quieter
"matched" simulator (seed 1005; its settings were chosen after Project 3 saw the deposit, so this
result is optimistic), scored on the real decisions.
